# Step back: the Grand Prix as a whole, and the UI (2026-11-11, v0.21.0)

The owner asked for two things: a look at what we've built as a whole, and a cleaner, more interesting UI.
**Report only: nothing here is built until the owner picks.** Screenshots: `~/Projects/swgr-underground/autotest/shots/`
`v20-home.png`, `v20-cups100.png`, `v20-cupresult.png`, `chain2.png` (v0.21 removes some of the text they show).

## 1. The whole thing

### What's working
- **The loop.** Class, then cup, then four races back to back with one button between them (NEXT RACE, no menu
  in between). The owner: "worked pretty well". This is the core of a kart game and it's there.
- **The AI is now measured, not guessed.** The study found the game's rubber band mostly *slows* the bots, so we
  tune that per class. The owner: "most of the wins felt earned". PHILOSOPHY point 1, the biggest risk, is in a
  good place.
- **Cups with a character per class:** forward layouts at 50cc, reverse at 100cc, weather at 150cc. The same cup
  feels new as you climb.
- **It's safe.** Its own save, the game's data put back after every change, the multiplayer guard, crash-safe
  restore on boot, 36 test files run before every version.
- **The standings page** (stakes line, near misses, record) is the strongest screen.

### What could be better
1. **We build faster than you can play.** 21 versions in three days. v0.21 isn't seen in game yet, and the open
   playtests (OWNER.md) go back to v0.17. Every untested change is a guess. Proposal: after the UI pass, a
   **feature freeze**: you race a full season (every cup at 100cc and 150cc) and we only fix what you find.
2. **The arc has no top.** Today a full run gives trophies, three titles and Kestar. There's no "I finished it"
   moment and nothing to come back for. The game ships 59 insignia icons and 59 insignia backgrounds
   (`Content/Insignias`): they're a ready-made set for the progress badges you asked for (TASKS round 2 item 2),
   e.g. one per gold cup, per title, a "perfect cup", "all gold at a class", "Kestar beaten".
3. **The garage may be a set-and-forget screen.** PHILOSOPHY point 5 hopes you'd pick HEAT SHIELD before a scorching
   cup. Do you ever change your abilities or tune-ups? If not, the garage could shrink to the racer pick, or the
   cups page could suggest the tune-up for that cup's weather.
4. **PHILOSOPHY.md is out of date.** Point 3 is the rival, now cut; the 200cc section says "cut it if it still
   feels silly", but you race it and enjoy it ("fast and brutal"). It's the yardstick, so it should say what you
   want now.
5. **Dead paths in the code.** The same-layout reset route (`ChainRaces.Reset`, off since it hung), study mode
   (done for now), old-save migrations, and switches kept for safety (`TileLabelStrip`, `PageBackdrop`). Each one is
   code that can break. Proposal: a tidy-up version that removes what's off and unused (study mode can move to
   tools/ like the recon spikes).

## 2. The UI

### What's wrong today
- **Every page looks the same:** title top left, yellow lines of text, a grid of same-size tiles in rows of four,
  left-aligned in a big panel. HOME, cups, garage, reset and the yes/no questions only differ in their words.
- **The text explains instead of showing.** Medals are written out ("BRONZE TROPHY") on tiles that also carry a
  medal icon. The cups page lists the focused cup's races with planet and lap count. The info lines repeat the
  tiles.
- **Nothing has a face.** The racers you race against, the leader in the title race and Kestar are only names.
- **The right half of the screen is empty** (the hangar droid shows through), while the tiles crowd the top left.

### What we can build with (the limits)
Only the game's own pieces: its tile button (any size), pictures from its texture files, text in its fonts, and
plain panels (rows, columns, boxes). No new art, no fonts of our own, no animation we've proven. That still allows
quite a lot: tiles of different sizes, big pictures, portraits, medals, and layout. The game has portraits for
several racers (`Content/Characters`: Lyren, Sen, Yara, Ziff, Ghost, Mercenary, the six player characters, Kestar
as `T_UI_Antagonist`) and big ability icons (`Icons/Abilities/LargeAbilityIcons`). Some decode badly in our
previews, so each needs a look in game.

### Proposal A: the quiet pass (text only, no new layout; small, low risk)
Cut what the screen already shows another way:
| Screen | Remove | Keep |
|---|---|---|
| HOME | "GALACTIC GRAND PRIX" (the tab already says GRAND PRIX), the info line "Parts level N, abilities level N.", RESET's "Asks twice" | the tiles; the lock reason on a locked class |
| Cups | "-  CUPS" in the title, "Best: BRONZE trophy", the medal word on tiles ("BRONZE TROPHY": the icon says it), "4 PLANETS", the planet and lap count in the race list | the four track names on one line (you pick tune-ups by them) |
| Garage | "Select: next racer.", "Levels up with the class (branches from 100cc).", the empty-slot sentence | the racer's vehicle, each pick's effect |
| Standings | "STANDINGS", the "You: 3rd in this race ..." line (your row shows it), the MOVED column when nobody moved | cup, track and race N of 4 on one line; the stakes line; near misses |
| Cup result | "Last race: ..." line | medal, top 3, points, NEW BEST |
| Title result | the second sentence on each outcome | the headline and your time |

### Proposal B: a layout per screen (bigger, after A; each screen checked in game)
Each screen gets one shape that suits it, so you can tell where you are at a glance:
```
HOME: four tall class posters, small strip below
 [      ][      ][      ][      ]
 [ 50CC ][100CC ][150CC ][200CC ]      each poster: the medal count as icons (gold/silver/bronze), not words
 [      ][      ][      ][      ]
 [GARAGE  your racer's portrait] [RESET]

CUPS: five planets in a row, the finale big, the title races with faces
 [JAKKU][LANTAANA][ANDO][CRAIT][SENTINEL]      medal icon only; no medal = never raced
 [      GALACTIC CUP       ][TITLE: portrait of the leader][KESTAR: his portrait]
 THE WAR FIELDS (REV) · GIANTS' GRAVEYARD · DEBRIS DUNES (REV) · SCRAP SPIRAL     (the focused cup's races)

GARAGE: the racer large on the left, the picks in a column on the right
 [  racer   ][ABILITY 1][ABILITY 2]
 [ portrait ][ABILITY 3][ABILITY 4]
 [          ][TUNE-UP 1][TUNE-UP 2]

CUP RESULT: centred, the medal huge, the top 3 as three portrait tiles (2nd 1st 3rd, podium order)
```
- **Faces:** the title tile shows the leader's portrait; Kestar's tile his; the cup result's podium shows the top 3.
  Where a racer has no portrait in the game, their racer-style picture (we use these in the garage already).
- **Medals as pictures everywhere**, never words. Fixes the gold vs bronze look-alike too (the tint or a bigger icon).
- **The panel goes**; the page is centred, and art fills the screen instead of a grey box.

### Proposal C: progress badges (round 2 item 2), once A and B are in
A column on the right of HOME using the game's insignias: one badge per achievement, dim until earned. That fills
the empty right half and answers "what's left to do" without a single line of text.

## Suggested order
1. A, the quiet pass (one version, quick to check).
2. B, one screen at a time: cups page first (most visited), then HOME, the cup result, the garage.
3. Then the feature freeze and your full season; badges (C) after that.
4. The tidy-up of dead code and PHILOSOPHY.md can go any time; it doesn't change what you see.

## 3. Claude's view on the garage after the cup (owner idea, 2026-11-11 late)
For it, with one change: **the garage stops being a HOME tile and becomes the cup's start screen.**
- HOME -> class -> cup -> start screen (racer, four abilities, two tune-ups, START). That's two menus below the
  cups page, the same depth as today's HOME -> GARAGE, so the "two menus deep" rule holds. HOME gets simpler
  (four classes and RESET).
- The cup's weather is on that screen ("SCORCHING"), right next to the tune-ups, so the heat / cold choice is made
  where it matters, with no sentence needed: a scorching cup shows a sun icon on HEAT SHIELD, for example.
- START is focused when the screen opens, with your last picks kept, so one press of A gets you racing. The extra
  press only costs anything when you choose to change something.
- Between cup races nothing changes: a cup keeps the loadout it started with (as today).
- The title race and Kestar get the same start screen.

On gating the tune-ups: if they aren't tied to the class, they're the obvious thing to earn, and that answers the
"no top" point. E.g. a tune-up for each gold at a class, or each title. Then the garage shows progress: locked packs
with "Gold in the Crait Cup" under them. Or keep them all open from the start and keep the unlocks for the badges.
