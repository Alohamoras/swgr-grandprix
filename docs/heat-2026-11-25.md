# HEAT: optional handicaps for Ace cups (plan, 2026-11-25)

Owner, 2026-11-25, in a planning chat about replay value: "the heat options sound brutal and fun, write up that work".
Status: **plan only, nothing built.** Work items: TASKS.md §1i. Open questions: OWNER.md.

## 1. Why
Every reward in the Grand Prix is a one-off: the leagues, the Galactic Cup, the three title races and then Kestar Bool.
After a gold in every Ace cup, nothing pulls you back except the racing itself. More unlocks would only make the ladder
longer. HEAT gives Ace a ceiling you keep raising yourself. Before a cup you switch on handicaps, each worth some heat.
A gold then counts at that heat, and each cup keeps your best (Hades' Heat, Slay the Spire's Ascension).

It serves PHILOSOPHY.md point 7 ("difficulty you choose, not difficulty that punishes"), point 4 (near misses:
"gold at heat 6, your best is 7") and point 6 (one goal always just out of reach). Unlocks stay as the climb through
Riff-Raff and Challenger. HEAT is what Ace is for once you get there.

## 2. The rules
- **Where:** Ace only, on every Ace points cup and the Galactic Cup. It is **not** on the Rally Cup (it has its own rules),
  title races or Kestar Bool at first (see §6).
- **When it opens:** your first gold at Ace, so the first trip through Ace is the plain league. *(Owner's call: or from
  the Ace title.)*
- **Picked before START** on the cup's start screen, the same screen that shows the four races and the tune-ups.
  Like the race list, it is fixed for the cup run (`run.heat`) and kept through the menu trips between races. Cups are
  not resumable, so leaving loses the run as it does today.
- **The score:** heat = the sum of the switched-on modifiers' points. The save keeps, per Ace cup, the best heat
  you won **gold** at (`heatBest.ace[cupKey]`). A silver or bronze at heat records nothing new. That keeps the target
  sharp: win it, then win it harder.
- **Shown:** a small heat number on the cup tile beside the medal (picture first; menu rules: no sentences). The game's
  CUP RESULT screen (cupend.lua) gets one tile line: `HEAT 7 - NEW BEST` or `HEAT 7 (best 9)`.
- **The garage and the AI put back as today:** every modifier writes memory at launch and is put back at the menu
  (house rules: no hooks, nothing kept between ticks).
- Heat never changes points, medals or unlocks. A gold at heat 0 is still a gold.

## 4. Screens
- **Start screen:** a HEAT tile under the tune-ups showing the current total (`HEAT 0`). Pressing it opens one panel
  (one level deep: within the two-menus rule) with a row per modifier (icon, name, tier pips, points) and the total.
  B goes back and keeps the picks. The last picks are remembered per league (`heatPicks.ace`) so "one more at +1" is
  one press.
- **Cups page:** the heat badge beside the medal on each Ace cup tile, plus the best of all Ace cups beside the league
  name on the Ace poster.
- **During the race:** nothing new (no HUD work). The standings panel's header carries `HEAT 7` so the stakes stay
  visible (PHILOSOPHY point 2).
- **CUP RESULT:** the line in §2. On a gold that beats your best, the trophy tile reads `NEW HEAT BEST`.

## 5. Save and code shape
- config.lua `Heat = { On = true, League = 'ace', OpensAt = 'gold', Mods = { { key = 'faster', tiers = { ... }, points = { 1, 2, 3 } }, ... } }`.
  Each modifier is a table of **overrides on existing dials**: profile fields, league fields (parts, ability level,
  mods), `AI.Grace`, AI tune-up tiers. A new `heat.lua` merges them over the league / profile **at launch only**, so
  nothing changes outside a heat cup and the put-back at the menu stays the one we already have.
- Save: `run.heat = { faster = 2, detuned = 1, ... }` (the run), `heatPicks[league]` (last picks), `heatBest[league][cup]`
  (best heat at gold). Register the fields in gp.lua `fill()`. An old save gets empty tables.
- prog.lua: `P.heatOpen(data)`; `P.nextGoal` may offer "Crait Cup: gold at heat 5 to beat your 4".
- Log: one `[heat]` line at launch (`[heat] ACE CRAIT CUP heat 7: faster 2, bare, grudge, armed`) next to the `[gaps]`
  lines, so a playtest log shows what was on.
- Tests (test/): merge order, stacking tiers, the totals, best-only-on-gold, the old save loading, Rally and title races
  untouched, every override put back.

## 7. Risks
- **Skill has to show** (PHILOSOPHY point 1). Heat only feels good if a cleaner, harder drive reliably wins more often.
  The owner's 2026-11-25 Ace log still had a wet sprint won by 20 s with the field crashing. Faster field and Grudge
  could make that variance worse, not harder. Watch the `[gaps]` spread at high heat.
- **Detuned and the bots:** the field copies your launch build (KNOWLEDGE §5), so lowering your parts lowers theirs
  unless `AI.Strength` / thrust keeps them where they were. This needs a check in the first held race.
- **Pressure above 1 is the game's own rubber band in places** (max time ahead / min behind): it may bring back the
  "close fight for 2nd whatever you do" feel that the honest pacing removed. If so, Faster field becomes thrust or
  part stacks instead.
