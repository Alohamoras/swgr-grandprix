# Galactic Grand Prix: research and recommendations (2026-11-10)

Markdown copy of the Claude Doc https://claude.ai/artifact/7VSJrJnpopuMcBFQ2WyTV8 (the doc is the one to comment on).
Answers to the owner's 13 questions, researched while they were out; proposed tasks are in TASKS.md ("Proposed").

## Summary
The mod's frame is right; what it needs is a flatter menu, honest AI at the top classes, and a champion to beat.

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| What else from the base game | Two unused tracks and ~45 unused weather / night / reverse variants (config lines only); the 11 rival 1 v 1s and the Showdown; per-track records. Knockouts, time trials and pods cost real work each. |
| What fits thematically | The Galactic League: classes as divisions, planet cups as a season, Derven as the finals, Kestar as the champion to dethrone. All flavour text and names are in the game. |
| Racer names / skills per league | Keep the real racers (voices, bios); the class sets their strength and the Elite tier carries mods from 150cc; a named champion per class. Renaming bots is possible but weak; true per-racer pace is a research spike. |
| Real progress | The rubber band keeps the AI at your pace. From 150cc make the AI honest (no slowing down when ahead) so a failed cup can be won later by being faster. Track records show progress even in a loss. |

Order: menu rework and the mods decision first, then class spacing and AI profiles, then records, title races, your racer, conditions cups.

## Theme and immersion
The Galactic League: Derven Acos is "home of the Galactic League, where racers face the ultimate challenge"; Kestar is its
champion; the campaign ends with "Defeated the Champion of the Galactic League". Classes = divisions, planet cups = a season,
Derven = the finals. In the files: planet flavour lines per planet; racer bios and ties (Nik Skandaro the Bools' former
enforcer, Biddy Blas under Kestar's banner, Sen Fira in debt to the Bools, Griff Halloran ex-Imperial Navy); the game's style
names (Engineer, Slipstreamer, Brawler, Ramjet Rider, Ice Cold, Firebug, Survivor, Tenacious, Redliner) for our mod groups;
three sponsors (The Broker, The Hutt, The Pirate). Gameplay: a season then the finals; the title race; a one-line intro per
class. Not: cutscenes, dialogue, paddock.

## Rosters per class and the champion race
Facts (menu dumps): the field is fixed: you plus the same 11 (character list `Opponents`); Shade never races because Shade
is the campaign's player character (`CharacterList.Player`); Kestar and Sebulba are never in the list. The campaign ranks
racers per tour: Loser (Ary Quill, Malis Vazosk), Riff-Raff (Nik Skandaro, Griff Halloran), Challenger (Goli), Elite (Sen
Fira, Biddy Blas, Lyren Shok), Ace (Kestar); Katja, Fola, Soren not loaded at the menu. In quick races the tiers do
nothing (shared pacing). Names are fixed on the racer defs; FreeRace renames your racer in memory (ChangeName /
PlayerNamePrivate), untried on bots. Per-racer mods are provable (bots copy the launch build's mods; we clear per bot).

| Option | Gets | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Same racers, class sets strength (ability level, Elite mods from 150cc, rubber band per class) | | 1-2 days | Low |
| B. Title holders per class | Champion named on the class tile and in the title race | half a day over the title race | None |
| C. Rename bots per class | Fictional rosters on HUD / standings | 1 day + race test | Medium (kits keyed by name; portraits / voices stay) |
| D. True per-racer skill | Fast and slow racers in one field | Unknown (campaign-only optional, unreadable) | High |

Recommend A + B; C only after playing A; D as a spike. Champion race: the Showdown per class works today (Kestar copies
your build); the class leader instead of Kestar via a two-car grid on a normal event + `CharacterList.Opponents` = one
racer (FreeRace does both for pods; unproven on cars), or rename Kestar's bot. Champion = most points across the five
planet cups at that class; fallback 50cc Griff Halloran, 100cc Lyren Shok, 150cc Biddy Blas, 200cc Kestar Bool. The
title race could be the gate to the next class.

## Difficulty and the feeling of progress
| Knob | Where | Default | The game's difficulties |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rubber band AI max / min time ahead | DA_RaceTuneConfig, shared by all 12 | 0 s, x1 | Cadet: max x0.65, min -3 s |
| Slowdown when ahead / catch-up when behind | DA_AIConfig pacing settings | 75% after 1.5 s ahead; 130% after 2 s behind | untouched (an "Honest" pacing asset exists) |
| Forced errors | DA_AIConfig | 15%/s in a zone 350 m ahead - 150 m behind, 8 s | untouched |
| Perfect start | DA_AIConfig | 75% | untouched |
| Aggression vs player | DA_AIConfig + rubber band multipliers | 50%, 2 s cooldown (100% vs AI) | untouched |
| Ability use | DA_AIAbility_* configs | Shockwave 100%, Vent 80%, Shield / Mine / Surge / Disruptor 50%, 5-15 s cooldowns | untouched (why Shields are rare, R7) |
| AI power level | campaign tune config | +0 | Ace +4, Champion +12 (x1.1 tour 3); campaign only |
| Crash allowance / full health at start | rubber band asset | 3, on | Cadet +1; Champion no reset |
| Per-racer tier | campaign AI build defs (TOptional) | | unreadable from Lua |

No difficulty menu: classes carry the profile (50cc Cadet, 100cc the game's own, 150cc honest pacing + errors halved +
abilities x1.25, 200cc honest + catch-up x1.3 + aggression x1.5 + abilities x1.5 + Elite mods); hidden `AI` knob in
config.lua. Honest pacing at the top is what makes "fail now, win later" real. Plus best times per track.

## Long-term grind
Gold everywhere (48 golds; count on the class tile), track records (best time / lap per track and class, NEW RECORD line),
title races, perfect-cup mark, conditions cups, pilot collection, a date-seeded challenge. Not: credits / shop, ghosts.
One number per class tile, one mark per cup tile, records on the cup page.

## Cup track lists
Config has path, name, kind; planet from the path; laps from GPRecon events.txt. Four lines on cup focus ("1 THE WAR FIELDS
- Jakku - circuit, 2 laps"); planet in the tile badge when no trophy / run.

## Your racer and your vehicle's look
Name = platform nickname; the pilot = `CharacterList.Player`; look = a recipe in the customisation subsystem (cloud-synced
save, never edit). FreeRace swaps the pilot before launch (put back once started), renames via ChangeName, clears the look
for the race. Recommend a RACER tile cycling the 16 pilots; pilot's name in our standings; no free-text; no cosmetics
(Kestar's white / gold is his pod's cockpit; every car shares one mesh; theme walk crashed; themes apply parts only).

## Vehicle mods
Trim to ~12 tune-ups picked in place (class sets 0 / 1 / 2 / 2), or cut; keep the code for the Elite AI's mods.

## Menu principles audit
Grid 4 x 3 = 12 tiles per page. Classes (1): 7 tiles. Cups (2): 13 tiles, scrolls once remix cups are open. Cup page (3).
Abilities (4) → pick (5). Mods (4) → group (5) → pick (6, 17 tiles, scrolls). Every screen has BACK / DONE / EXIT.
Proposal: level 1 = classes + GARAGE + CONTINUE CUP; level 2 = cups (track list on focus, START on select, NEXT RACE on the
in-progress tile) and GARAGE (vehicle / ability 1-4 / mod 1-4 / racer, cycling in place). Cut EXIT, BACK, DONE, the points
line, "Select to change".
