GALACTIC GRAND PRIX - CHANGELOG
(Nexus makes one bullet per line. Paste each version under its own entry in Manage > Changelogs.)

v0.45.2

New podracing league. A fourth poster on the home page opens the Podracer Cup: four races on the pod tracks of Mos Espa, Mawhonic and Holdfast's Folly against Sebulba, the other pod racers and Kestar Bool. You fly your own pilot's podracer.
Podracing has two levels. Learner has no abilities. Pro puts every pod at level 7 parts and gives every racer, you included, their abilities. Each level keeps its own trophy and lap records.
The Galactic Cup now has a finale. Win a trophy in it and Kestar Bool challenges you to a one-on-one race in the stadium for the league title. Beat him at Ace and you can race as him. The separate title race is gone.
Your best lap on each track is shown during the start countdown, and your best lap of the race is shown on the standings.
The cup result now uses the game's own end-of-tour screen, showing the planets you raced, how you placed on each, and your trophy.
A cup where you won every race gets a crown.
The racers ahead of you no longer fire their boost every time you fire yours. The racers behind you catch up harder instead, so a lead has to be earned.
The computer racers now use their abilities.
The first 15 seconds of a race are a grace period, so the pack does not fly away at the green light.
The computer racers' cars are stronger: a little at Junker, more at Challenger, most at Ace.
Each league has its own car setup. Junker has a maxed afterburner and ramjet on an otherwise stock car. Challenger has every part at level 7. Ace has every part at level 10. This applies to you and the computer racers alike.
The Rally Cup's rivals now keep up instead of falling far behind.
The precision buoys no longer appear on The Galactic Gauntlet in cup races.
All the menu text has been rewritten in plain English. Every locked item says what you need to do to unlock it.
Menus use the game's own fonts and colours, and the D-pad moves between tiles correctly on every page.
The Start button is disabled while the game's menu is locked by an online queue.
The cup result screen now closes when you press any button. It used to wait two minutes.
Pressing a button while the next race loads can no longer start the campaign by mistake.
Your save from an earlier version still works.

v0.31.1

New Rally Cup. Every point-to-point road in the game, run as a seven-stage rally: The Giants' Graveyard, Geothermal Tour, Rhodochrosite Run, Bool River Run in both directions, Mountain to Sea, and Dai Bendu Ascension at night as the finale.
The rally is four cars: you and three rivals picked when the cup starts, the same three every stage.
Rally standings are by total time. If you do not finish a stage you get the slowest finisher's time plus 30 seconds.
The weather and time of day for each rally stage are hidden until the stage loads.
The Rally Cup does not count towards unlocking leagues or the Galactic Cup.
The garage now has a Pilot tile showing the racer's face and a Vehicle tile. Any pilot can drive a landspeeder, speeder bike or skim speeder. A pilot's own vehicle looks as it does in the game; the other two use that rider's colour scheme.
Each pilot starts with their own four abilities. You can still change them, and your saved choices from earlier versions are kept.
The title race and the final boss use whichever vehicle you picked.
Thanks to MilkManEX for the pilot faces, the vehicle choice and the signature abilities (pull request #1).
