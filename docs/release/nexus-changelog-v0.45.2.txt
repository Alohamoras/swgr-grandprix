v0.45.2 - PODRACING, the Galactic Cup finale, your lap records in the race, a faster field, new menu text

NEW: PODRACING
A fourth poster on the home page, a league of its own. The PODRACER CUP: four races drawn
from the pod tracks of Mos Espa, Mawhonic and Holdfast's Folly, against Sebulba, the other
pod racers and Kestar Bool. You fly your pilot's own podracer (Shade, Goli, Fola, Sebulba,
or Kestar once you have beaten him). Two levels, each with its own trophy and records:
- LEARNER: no abilities, the pace to learn the tracks.
- PRO: every pod at level 7 parts, your four abilities on your pod, and every pod racer
  carries a kit of their own.

NEW: THE GALACTIC CUP FINALE
Take a trophy in the Galactic Cup and Kestar Bool waits as its fifth race: a 1 v 1 in the
stadium for the league title. Beat him at ACE and he races for you. The separate title race
tile is gone; the league's gold star now means its title.

NEW: IN THE RACE AND ON THE RESULTS
- PREVIOUS BEST LAP during the start countdown: your lap record on this track at this league.
- Your best lap on the standings, NEW BEST LAP when it falls, and the near-miss lines for both
  the race record and the lap record.
- The CUP RESULT on the game's own tour-end screen: the planets you raced with your finish on
  each, your trophy and standing. A crown on a cup you won every race of.
- Standings header, stakes and notes reworded by the mod's author in plain words: "4 POINTS
  AHEAD OF SEN FIRA, 2 RACES LEFT", "GOLD SECURED", "NO TROPHY POSSIBLE", "NEW TRACK RECORD".

THE FIELD
- Boosting feels fast: the racers ahead of you no longer fire their ramjet in reply to yours
  (they did 75% of the time); the racers behind you catch up harder instead.
- The bots use their abilities (kits per pilot, cycled in the race), with a 15 s grace at
  the start so the field does not launch away at the green light.
- Pilot strength per league: JUNKER +3 parts around, CHALLENGER +5, ACE +8 (the Elite a step
  more, the weak a step less). The back of the grid stays in the race (no slow row).
- The car per league: JUNKER's afterburner and ramjet at 10 on stock everything else,
  CHALLENGER every part at 7, ACE every part at 10 - yours and theirs.
- The Rally Cup's rivals catch up (they used to fall 15-60 s back).
- The Precision buoys are off The Galactic Gauntlet when it is raced as a cup race.

MENUS
- Every line on screen rewritten by the mod's author: plain statements, what each thing is
  and how it unlocks. The league's rules under the focused poster; "Obtain Five JUNKER
  trophies to unlock." on a locked league; the game's own typefaces and ink on our text.
- The D-pad follows the tiles' real positions on every page.
- START refuses while the game's menu is locked by an online queue.

FIXES
- The cup result screen closes on A, Enter, Space, B or Escape (it used to wait 120 s).
- A press while the next race loads can no longer start the campaign.

Requires UE4SS for Galactic Racer (Open Beta v0.9.4 or later). Offline only. Your save from
an earlier version is kept; a locked pilot you were racing as falls back to Shade.
