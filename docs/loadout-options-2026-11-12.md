# Making the loadout choice matter: options (2026-11-12)

For the owner to pick from (TASKS.md section 4, "Tune-ups gated by league" and "Heat / cold and the packs"). Nothing
built.

## The problem
- **HEAT SHIELD is almost always the pick.** It makes you immune to Burning AND Frozen, so it covers both kinds of
  weather in one slot. In today's logs the owner raced with it in nearly every cup, and the Elite carry it too. A choice
  with one answer isn't a choice.
- **The weather rarely matters.** Of the 81 races in the random pools, 5 are SCORCHING and 9 SNOW; the rest are clear,
  night, wet, mines, overcast or storm. A four-race cup usually has none or one weather race.
- **Unknown:** whether SCORCHING / SNOW actually burn or freeze your car on their own, or only the abilities do
  (Vent Flame, Frozen mines). One race with the log open would tell.
- **Tune-ups come with the league** (Riff-Raff 0 slots, Challenger 1, Ace 2): nothing to earn, so nothing to chase once
  a league is open.

## Options (they combine)

**A. Split HEAT SHIELD** (cheap, config only). Two packs: FIRE GUARD (Burning) and FROST GUARD (Frozen), each with a
second small perk so neither is dead weight. The start screen already names the cup's conditions, so you pick for the
cup you drew. Downside: with weather in only 1 race of 4, often neither matters - see C.

**B. Earn the tune-ups** (moderate). Each planet cup's GOLD (any league) unlocks one pack, themed to the planet:
Jakku (scorching) -> FIRE GUARD, Ando Prime (snow) -> FROST GUARD, Crait (mines) -> SURVIVOR, Sentinel I (acid) ->
TENACIOUS, Lantaana (wet / lava) -> ICE COLD; the Galactic Cup gold -> one of the strong ones (REDLINER or
AFTERBURNER). The slots stay by league. Gives a reason to go back for golds after a league opens - the "something to
chase" gap.

**C. Weather that shows up** (cheap, config). Lean the draw so each planet cup has at least one condition race (a
Jakku cup always has one SCORCHING, an Ando cup one SNOW) - the owner's "leaning" idea, by planet instead of by league.
Then A's choice is real in every cup.

**D. Rebalance the rest** (cheap, config). Look at the twelve packs for ones nobody would take and give them a reason
(e.g. SURVIVOR on crash-heavy tracks, SLIPSTREAMER in a full grid). Best done after a few cups with A-C in place.

## Claude's pick
**A + C first** (both config: a real weather choice every cup), then **B** (golds unlock packs) as the long-run reward.
D after the owner has raced them. Before A: one race on a SCORCHING and one on a SNOW track with no tune-up, to see
whether the weather burns / freezes you by itself (if it doesn't, A needs a different hook - e.g. packs vs the
abilities the field carries).
