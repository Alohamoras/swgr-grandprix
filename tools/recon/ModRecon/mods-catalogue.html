Mods catalogue

Every vehicle mod (the game calls them affixes) read from the game, grouped by what it affects, with the real numbers for each step. Each one is tagged: mods that change how you race stay in, mods that only make you faster are left out. Use the filters to skim, and tell me any you'd rescue or drop.

What the test showed

Mods work

Two mods written onto the landspeeder's launch build (Improved Regulators IV, Frost Protection) became active passives on the car in the race.

Yours only is possible

The AI cars copy the same build, so they got them too. Clearing the bots' copies at race load left them with none and kept yours.

The rule: change how you race, not how fast

Protection / rule-changer

mods. Change what can hurt you or how a system behaves: Frost Protection, Antacid Coating (Corroded no longer causes Choked), Uncapped Cooling. Good picks for a planet's hazards.

Playstyle

mods. A bonus for driving a certain way: Cooling Slipstream, Shield Cycler, Reconstituted Fuel on a perfect landing. are marked check: big speed or power payoffs to try in a race before keeping.

Power left out

mods. Always-on "more of a number": Enriched Fuel, Improved Regulators, Ramjet Power, Faster Attachments, speed that stacks per takedown or landing. Always the right pick, so no real choice, and they widen the gap to the AI.
